What it won't read

Threats or harassment. Attacks on a named private person. Anything encouraging self-harm. Me making commitments, quoting rates, or endorsing things I have not endorsed. Phone numbers, addresses, or other personal information. Everything else, including opinions I don't share, is fair game. If a script is refused, the avatar tells you why.

Privacy

edzanelli.com is my personal site. It does not use cookies, analytics, or forms, and it stores nothing about you.

The Ask Ed page runs an interactive avatar provided by HeyGen (LiveAvatar). When you start a session, your browser asks for microphone permission and your voice is sent to HeyGen so the avatar can hear and answer you. HeyGen processes the audio and keeps a transcript of the session, which I can review to improve the avatar's answers. The text of the conversation is also sent to the model provider you select (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google) to generate the answer. Do not share anything in a session that you would not want in a transcript.

Each provider's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy. edzanelli.com receives no audio and no transcript.

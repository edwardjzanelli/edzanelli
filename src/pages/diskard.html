Diskard

Black out what you’re sick of seeing.

I built Diskard because some headlines make me feel dumber just for having read them, like any and all references to the Kardashians.

How it works

Type the words or names you never want to see again, one per line. Diskard blacks out matching stories, posts and videos on the pages you visit. Changed your mind about one? Click Reveal.

What it costs

Nothing. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Nothing you block ever leaves your browser. Privacy policy

Where to get it

Coming soon to Chrome, Edge and Brave. Safari after that.
